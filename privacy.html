Privacy Policy

JillCal · Last updated November 24, 2026

JillCal does not collect or share any of your personal information. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any server the developer runs. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Your calendars

With your permission, JillCal reads and edits the events in the calendars on your iPhone, using Apple's Calendar system. Your events stay in those calendars and are handled by whichever account they belong to (for example iCloud or Google). JillCal never sends your events anywhere else.

Event colors and settings

To remember your colors, JillCal saves a small list on your iPhone: the event titles you've used with the color you picked for each, the color you picked for a single event (linked by the event's identifier), and when you last used it. Your settings are saved on your iPhone too.

If iCloud is on, that color list is also kept in your own private iCloud storage, so your colors come back after a reinstall or on a new iPhone. Only you can access that private iCloud data. The developer cannot see it.

Your choices

You can turn off JillCal's access to your calendars at any time in the Settings app, under Apps > JillCal > Calendars. Deleting JillCal removes everything it saved on your iPhone. To remove the copy in iCloud, open the Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud > Manage Storage (or Storage), choose JillCal if it is listed, and delete its data.

Sharing

JillCal does not share or sell any data, and it does not use any third-party services.

Children

JillCal does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
