Privacy Policy

JillCal · Last updated November 11, 2026

JillCal does not collect, store or share any of your personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Your calendars

With your permission, JillCal reads and edits the events in the calendars on your iPhone, using Apple's Calendar system. Your events stay in those calendars and are handled by whichever account they belong to (for example iCloud or Google). JillCal never sends your events anywhere else.

Event colors and settings

The colors you choose for events, and your settings, are saved on your iPhone. Colors are also kept in your own private iCloud storage so they come back after a reinstall or on a new iPhone. Only you can access that private iCloud data. The developer cannot see it.

Sharing

JillCal does not share or sell any data, and it does not use any third-party services.

Children

JillCal does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
