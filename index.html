My Google or iCloud calendars don't show up

JillCal shows the calendars that are already on your iPhone. To add an account, open the Settings app, go to Apps > Calendar > Calendar Accounts > Add Account, choose Google or iCloud, sign in and turn on Calendars. Then open JillCal again.

JillCal says it needs my calendars

Open the Settings app, go to Apps > JillCal > Calendars, and choose Full Access.

Are my events backed up?

Your events stay in your own calendars (iCloud, Google or another account), so they're backed up by that account. Events in a calendar that's only "On this iPhone" aren't backed up. The colors you pick for events are saved on your iPhone and in your own private iCloud.
